# Rulebook: Swiss students moving to St.Gallen

Built 9 Oct 2026 from official pages only (city of St.Gallen, canton of St.Gallen, ch.ch, HSG). Every row quotes the source sentence, in the original language. "not in source" means the cited page does not say it. Quotes ending in "…" were cut short by the page extractor; open the URL for the full sentence. Before Demo Day, spot-check a few quotes against the live page.

**Profiles used in the "Who" column**
- **Swiss – main residence**: Swiss citizen who moves their main residence (Hauptwohnsitz) to St.Gallen.
- **Swiss – weekly resident**: Swiss student who keeps their main residence in another Swiss municipality and registers St.Gallen as a secondary residence (Wochenaufenthalter/Nebenwohnsitz).
- **Swiss men (service-liable)**: Swiss men liable to military service (Wehrpflicht). Swiss women only if they volunteered.
- **Everyone**: all people moving to St.Gallen.

## Missing for a complete checklist

1. **Fees for Swiss registration**: neither city page gives the amount for main or secondary residence (only "kostenpflichtig" and fee schedule SRS 416.3).
2. **Main residence vs weekly resident decision rule**: no source says which students must take main residence in St.Gallen and which may stay weekly residents.
3. **Military reporting conflict**: canton (S4) says no separate report to the Kreiskommando; HSG (S7) says report to the Sektionschef. Needs confirmation from Kreiskommando (kreiskommando@sg.ch, +41 58 229 71 71). The canton page is likely newer, but that is inferred.
4. **Exemption tax for students**: no source says how students with low or no income are assessed, beyond the CHF 400 minimum, or when the invoice arrives.
5. **Civil protection after moving**: no source says how assignment to the St.Gallen civil protection organisation works after a move.
6. **Civil service duties on moving**: zivi.admin.ch was not read; reporting duties for civil servants are not covered.
7. **Health insurance when moving within Switzerland**: whether a change of insurer region/premium region is required is not in source.
8. **Tax**: no tax rules for weekly residents vs main residents were read.
9. **Integration for Swiss newcomers**: the official integration pages target migrants; no city welcome event or orientation for Swiss newcomers was found.
