# Studying at HSG as a UK citizen — Step-by-step admin guide

## At a glance

A UK citizen coming to study at HSG is treated as a **third-country (non-EU/EFTA) national**: the residence permit must be **granted before entry**, and the application goes to the St.Gallen Migrationsamt **at least 2 months before studies start**.

- **Who this covers:** holders of a British passport with nationality "British Citizen", enrolling in a degree programme at HSG (Bachelor, Master, PhD) and living in the City of St.Gallen. Exchange students follow a separate HSG-run process (noted where it differs).
- **Visa:** British Citizens need **no visa**, even for stays over 90 days ([SEM, list V2](https://www.sem.admin.ch/sem/en/home/publiservice/weisungen-kreisschreiben/visa/liste1_staatsangehoerigkeit/leg_visum/v2.html)). Other British passport types (B.N.O., B.O.T.C., B.O.C., British Subject, British Protected Person) **do need a visa** for stays over 90 days and must apply through a Swiss embassy or consulate instead.
- **Permit:** studying longer than 3 months requires a residence permit. Visa-exempt applicants can send the application from abroad directly to the cantonal migration office and must wait for the decision abroad ([Merkblatt Studium/Doktorat Drittstaaten](https://www.sg.ch/content/dam/sgch/sicherheit/migration/dokumente_einreise,-aufenhalt-und-ausreise/drittstaaten/einreise-ohne-erwerb-drittstaaten/sch%C3%BCler---studenten/Merkblatt%20Studium%20Doktorat%20Drittstaaten.pdf), §4).
- **After arrival:** register in person at the Residents' Office within **14 days**; late registration is fined.
- **Work:** no automatic right to work. A side job of max. 15 h/week can be approved at the earliest **6 months after studies start**, and only after an application.

Every step below names the office, the documents and the deadline, and links the official page it comes from. Points the sources leave open are flagged as open questions in the last section.

## Timeline

Everything hinges on one date: the permit application must reach the Migrationsamt **at least 2 months before studies start**. For the autumn semester (lectures began 14 Sep in 2026, with Start Week 7–11 Sep, per [HSG semester dates](https://www.unisg.ch/de/studium/studieren-an-der-hsg/semesterdaten/)), that means early July at the latest.

## Step by step: before you leave the UK

The critical path is the residence-permit application: it needs your HSG enrolment confirmation, must reach the Migrationsamt **at least 2 months before your studies start**, and you must wait for the decision in the UK.

1. **Apply to HSG and get admitted.** HSG degree studies generally start in the autumn semester; Bachelor applications for the autumn semester run 1 Oct – 30 Apr, Master deadlines differ by programme ([HSG application deadlines](https://www.unisg.ch/de/studium/zulassung/anmeldefristen/)). Your official admission decision (Zulassungsverfügung) arrives only in your Compass inbox, not by post ([HSG, Wohnen in St.Gallen](https://www.unisg.ch/de/studium/studieren-an-der-hsg/wohnen-in-stgallen/)).
2. **Check your passport type.** If the nationality field says "British Citizen", no visa is needed. Any other British nationality entry requires a national visa D, applied for in person at the Swiss representation in your country of residence ([SEM V2](https://www.sem.admin.ch/sem/en/home/publiservice/weisungen-kreisschreiben/visa/liste1_staatsangehoerigkeit/leg_visum/v2.html)). Your passport should also stay valid at least 3 months beyond your planned stay, since that is required when the permit is extended ([hallo.sg.ch](https://www.hallo.sg.ch/de/zusammenleben/leben-in-st-gallen/aufenthaltsbewilligung/_jcr_content/pdfonthefly.ocFile/aufenthaltsbewilligung-de.pdf)).
3. **Collect the permit documents** ([Merkblatt Studium/Doktorat Drittstaaten](https://www.sg.ch/content/dam/sgch/sicherheit/migration/dokumente_einreise,-aufenhalt-und-ausreise/drittstaaten/einreise-ohne-erwerb-drittstaaten/sch%C3%BCler---studenten/Merkblatt%20Studium%20Doktorat%20Drittstaaten.pdf), §3):
   - Form A1 (Gesuch Ausländerbewilligung), completed
   - certificates of completed school, university or vocational education
   - enrolment confirmation (Immatrikulationsbestätigung) from HSG; request it from the Dean's Advisory Office if you need it early
   - proof of funds: confirmation from a bank or PostFinance domiciled in Switzerland showing at least **CHF 2,000 per month**, or a guarantee from a solvent person living in Switzerland (with their latest tax bill and debt-collection extract)
   - copy of your valid passport
   - **translations** of every document not written in German. This includes English-language certificates.
4. **Submit the application to the Migrationsamt St.Gallen from the UK**, at least 2 months before your studies start. Applications from people already in Switzerland are not accepted, and the procedure must be awaited abroad (Merkblatt §4). Online submission runs through the canton's [eMigrationsamt](https://emigrationsamt.sg.ch/) counter ([sg.ch](https://www.sg.ch/sicherheit/einreise-aufenthalt-ausreise/bewilligugnen-eu-efta.html)).
5. **Wait for the assurance of a residence permit** (Zusicherung der Aufenthaltsbewilligung). Do not travel to settle in St.Gallen before you have it: the permit must be issued before entry ([hallo.sg.ch](https://www.hallo.sg.ch/de/zusammenleben/leben-in-st-gallen/aufenthaltsbewilligung/_jcr_content/pdfonthefly.ocFile/aufenthaltsbewilligung-de.pdf)).
6. **Find housing.** Have ready a copy of your permit and ID, an employment contract if any, and a credit or debt-collection report. Expect a deposit of up to **3 months' rent**, paid into a blocked account in your name; some landlords also require liability and household insurance ([HSG, Wohnen in St.Gallen](https://www.unisg.ch/de/studium/studieren-an-der-hsg/wohnen-in-stgallen/)). You need a rental agreement or accommodation confirmation to register on arrival.
7. **Decide your health-insurance route** before you travel (details in step 12): keep or apply for a UK GHIC/EHIC, or line up a Swiss student package. Travel insurance is not accepted.
8. **Budget.** HSG cites a monthly budget of roughly **CHF 2,200–2,600** for living costs ([HSG](https://www.unisg.ch/de/studium/studieren-an-der-hsg/wohnen-in-stgallen/)). Keep cash or a card for the registration fees on arrival.

**Exchange students** skip steps 3–5: HSG Student Mobility applies for the permit grant and e-mails it (autumn semester: by end of June; spring: mid-November). You then reimburse **CHF 96** online by credit card before arrival, even if you later cancel ([HSG exchange guide](https://www.unisg.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/HSG_ROOT/_Kernauftritt_HSG/Studium/Austauschprogramme/Incoming_Gaststudierende/01_EN-staymorethan90Days_abJan26_final.pdf)).

## Step by step: arrival and the first 14 days

Within **14 days of arrival** you must register in person at the Residents' Office (Bevölkerungsdienste) of the City of St.Gallen; late registration is fined ([HSG, Wohnen in St.Gallen](https://www.unisg.ch/de/studium/studieren-an-der-hsg/wohnen-in-stgallen/)).

9. **Enter Switzerland** with your British passport (and visa D, if your passport type needs one).
10. **Register at the Residents' Office**, Rathaus, Poststrasse 28, 9001 St.Gallen ([City of St.Gallen](https://www.stadt.sg.ch/home/welcome/residence-registration/moving-and-registering.html)). Bring:
    - passport (foreign nationals present their identity and travel documents)
    - rental agreement or confirmation from your accommodation provider
    - valid health-insurance card or current insurance policy
    - the assurance of a residence permit (Zusicherung)
    - money for the registration fees, payable in cash or by card
11. **Attend the biometrics appointment.** After registration the Migrationsamt sends an invitation by post. At the Ausweisstelle your photo, signature and, if required, fingerprints are taken. Bring the invitation letter and your passport, and come only on the date in the letter; it can be rescheduled online ([sg.ch](https://www.sg.ch/sicherheit/einreise-aufenthalt-ausreise/bewilligugnen-eu-efta.html)). The residence-permit card then arrives by post at your St.Gallen address.

**Fees and timing published for UK exchange students** (degree-student amounts are not published in the sources): **CHF 123** for the permit card plus **CHF 25** processing fee at the Residents' Office, then **CHF 25** for biometrics at the Migrationsamt. The card should arrive about **10 days** after biometrics. Exchange students also bring HSG Form R and upload it, stamped, to Mobility-Online ([HSG exchange guide](https://www.unisg.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/HSG_ROOT/_Kernauftritt_HSG/Studium/Austauschprogramme/Incoming_Gaststudierende/01_EN-staymorethan90Days_abJan26_final.pdf)).

## Step by step: the first months

The one hard deadline after registration is health insurance: proof of cover or an exemption must be filed **within 3 months of arrival**.

12. **Health insurance: file within 3 months** ([HSG health-insurance fact sheet](https://www.unisg.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/HSG_ROOT/_Kernauftritt_HSG/Studium/Austauschprogramme/Incoming_Gaststudierende/IN_FactSheet_HealthInsurance_morethan90days_ENG_30012026.pdf), Jan 2026). Swiss insurance is compulsory for anyone living in Switzerland more than 3 months. UK students have three routes:
    - **UK GHIC or UK EHIC:** apply for exemption anyway. Submit the completed exemption form, a copy of the card and your HSG enrolment confirmation at the Residents' Office or by e-mail to kvg@stadt.sg.ch. The exemption only holds while you are **not working** in Switzerland.
    - **Swiss student package** (e.g. Swisscare, Scorestudies): also needs the exemption form, with the Swiss insurer's confirmation on page 2.
    - **Standard Swiss basic insurance (KVG)**, chosen yourself.
    - Travel insurance is not accepted. If the exemption is not completed, you can be assigned a regular Swiss policy, which is far more expensive ([FOPH](https://www.bag.admin.ch/en/health-insurance-foreign-students-in-switzerland)).
13. **Working while studying: only with prior approval.** For third-country students, a side job of max. **15 h/week** can be approved at the earliest **6 months after studies start**, if the university confirms it will not prolong your studies; work in your academic field can be approved earlier. An application must be filed **before** you start the job ([Merkblatt Studium/Doktorat Drittstaaten](https://www.sg.ch/content/dam/sgch/sicherheit/migration/dokumente_einreise,-aufenhalt-und-ausreise/drittstaaten/einreise-ohne-erwerb-drittstaaten/sch%C3%BCler---studenten/Merkblatt%20Studium%20Doktorat%20Drittstaaten.pdf), §5); HSG names the cantonal Amt für Wirtschaft und Arbeit as the office ([HSG](https://www.unisg.ch/de/studium/studieren-an-der-hsg/wohnen-in-stgallen/)). Taking a job also ends a GHIC/EHIC health-insurance exemption (step 12).
14. **Social security (AHV).** Foreign students who do not work are insured and must contribute only if their civil-law domicile is in Switzerland. Non-earners contribute from 1 January after turning 20 ([HSG](https://www.unisg.ch/de/studium/studieren-an-der-hsg/wohnen-in-stgallen/)).
15. **Keep your address current.** Any later move within or out of the city must be reported within 14 days ([City of St.Gallen](https://www.stadt.sg.ch/home/welcome/residence-registration/moving-and-registering.html)).

## Step by step: during your studies and when you leave

The permit has to be extended at the Migrationsamt **at least 2 weeks before it expires**, every time it runs out.

16. **Extend the residence permit.** File the extension with the Migrationsamt at least 2 weeks before expiry. Attach the current permit, your passport (valid at least 3 months beyond the end of the stay) and the expiry notice if you received one ([hallo.sg.ch](https://www.hallo.sg.ch/de/zusammenleben/leben-in-st-gallen/aufenthaltsbewilligung/_jcr_content/pdfonthefly.ocFile/aufenthaltsbewilligung-de.pdf)).
17. **If you lose the permit card:** report it in person at any Swiss police station, then send the written loss report to the Migrationsamt or hand it in at the counter and ask for a duplicate ([sg.ch](https://www.sg.ch/sicherheit/einreise-aufenthalt-ausreise/bewilligugnen-eu-efta.html)).
18. **If you move to another canton:** third-country nationals with a B or L permit must apply for the canton change **before** moving; L-permit holders have no entitlement to it ([Merkblatt Kantonswechsel](https://www.sg.ch/content/dam/sgch/sicherheit/migration/dokumente_einreise,-aufenhalt-und-ausreise/kantonswechsel/Merkblatt%20Kantonswechsel.pdf), §2.3).
19. **After graduating:** graduates of a Swiss university can be admitted for **6 months** to look for a qualified job, if they have enough funds and suitable housing. This permit cannot be extended (Merkblatt Studium/Doktorat Drittstaaten, §6).
20. **Deregister when you leave Switzerland.** Deregistration abroad is only possible in person at the Residents' Office counter. Bring the tax office's notice of departure and your original permit card; visit the city tax office (City Hall, 2nd floor) **one month before** moving, with the questionnaire "Moving abroad" ([City of St.Gallen](https://www.stadt.sg.ch/home/welcome/residence-registration/moving-and-registering.html)). Exchange students instead use HSG Form D, stamped at the Residents' Office up to one month before departure and uploaded to Mobility-Online ([Form D](https://www.unisg.ch/fileadmin/user_upload/HSG_ROOT/_Kernauftritt_HSG/Studium/Austauschprogramme/Incoming_Gaststudierende/Form_D_January_2026.pdf)).

## Open points and sources

The official pages leave seven points open; confirm them with the Migrationsamt (+41 58 229 36 90) or HSG before the guide goes live.

- **Fees for degree students:** only exchange-student amounts are published (CHF 123 + 25 + 25). Fees for the permit assurance and for degree students' B permit are not stated.
- **Permit type and validity:** the sources do not say whether UK degree students get a B or L permit, or for how long it is issued.
- **Processing time** of the permit application: not stated; only the 2-month minimum lead time is.
- **Proof-of-funds amount:** the canton asks for ≥ CHF 2,000/month; HSG's visa guidance cites CHF 24,000. Clarify which applies to visa-exempt UK citizens.
- **Translations:** the canton requires German translations of all non-German documents; confirm whether English certificates are accepted untranslated.
- **AHV:** whether a UK student counts as having civil-law domicile in Switzerland, and how to register and pay.
- **Not covered by any source used:** Swiss bank account, taxes, broadcasting fee, mobile phone, family members joining.
