# Rulebook: EU/EFTA students moving to St.Gallen

Version 3, 10 Oct 2026. Scope: EU/EFTA citizens only; UK and other countries are out of scope. Built from official pages only (city of St.Gallen, canton of St.Gallen incl. hallo.sg.ch, federal SEM/AHV/Federal Council, HSG), plus Serafe (the federal fee agency). Every row quotes the source sentence in its original language. "not in source" means the cited page does not say it; no gaps were filled from general knowledge. Quotes are short exact fragments (the page extractor caps quote length). Before Demo Day, spot-check a few quotes against the live page.

**Purpose:** raw data for the profile-based website. Sections A–H are the core process; I covers country-specific differences; J–M cover studies, money, daily life and health; N is a chronological checklist that maps each step to its row.

**Profiles used in the "Who" column**
- **EU/EFTA – all**: any EU/EFTA citizen studying in St.Gallen.
- **EU/EFTA – degree**: enrolled in a full degree programme at HSG (Bachelor/Master/PhD).
- **EU/EFTA – exchange >90 days**: incoming exchange student staying more than 90 days (one semester or longer).
- **EU/EFTA – under 3 months**: stays of 90 days / 3 months or less.
- **EU/EFTA – working**: students with a paid job or paid internship in Switzerland.
- **EU/EFTA – commuter**: student who lives in a neighbouring EU country and returns home daily (e.g. from Germany or Austria).
- **Country groups** (section I): Croatia; Germany/France/Italy/Austria; Liechtenstein; Iceland/Norway; "EU-15/EFTA" vs newer EU states.

Stay length: the sources split at **3 months / 90 days**. The only **1 year** line is SEM: permits are issued for the study duration "or for one year" (row C5). The **5 year** line matters for PhD students (C permit, row I6).

**Website filters these rows support:** nationality group · degree vs exchange · stay length · working or not · living in St.Gallen vs commuting.
