# St.Gallen study guide: students from other countries (non-EU/EFTA, non-UK)

This covers people coming to study at the University of St.Gallen (HSG) from outside the EU/EFTA, excluding the UK. Purpose of stay: study. Collected 2026-10-10. Every fact has a source, listed at the bottom.

---

## 1. Which route applies

### Visa or no visa (stays over 90 days)
| Group | Countries | What they need |
|---|---|---|
| **No visa** | Andorra, Australia, Brunei, Japan, Malaysia, Monaco, New Zealand, San Marino, Singapore, Vatican City | Permit only. Apply from abroad directly to the Migrationsamt St.Gallen [S10, S9] |
| **Visa D** | Every other non-EU country | National visa D, applied for at the Swiss embassy/consulate [S10, S9] |

The following are visa-free for short trips but **need a visa D to study**: USA, Canada, Brazil, South Korea, Ukraine, Hong Kong, Taiwan, Macao [S10]. A tourist entry cannot be switched to a study stay [S9: the decision must be awaited abroad].

### Exchange students: three HSG routes [S14]
| Route | Countries | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Permit grant** | Australia, Japan, Malaysia, New Zealand, Singapore (+ UK) | HSG applies for the permit grant. Student needs a valid passport |
| **Visa in person** | Afghanistan, Algeria, Bangladesh, Burundi, Cameroon, DR Congo, Guinea, Haiti, Iran, Iraq, Libya, Morocco, Myanmar, Nepal, North Korea, Pakistan, Republic of the Congo, Rwanda, Sri Lanka, Sudan, South Sudan, Syria, Vietnam (23) | Student applies in person at the Swiss embassy/consulate with the HSG acceptance letter, "as soon as possible" after acceptance |
| **Visa grant** | All other countries | HSG applies for the visa grant. Student then contacts the consulate named on the document |

## 2. Checklist: degree students (Bachelor, Master, PhD)

**Permit type:** B, renewed every year [S6].

### Before arrival
1. **Apply for the permit at least 2 months before the course starts** [S9]. Visa-required nationals apply at the Swiss representation. Visa-exempt nationals apply directly to the Migrationsamt, also online at emigrationsamt.sg.ch [S9, S16]. You cannot apply from inside Switzerland or through someone living in Switzerland, and you wait for the decision abroad [S9].
2. **Documents with Form A1** [S9]:
   - Proof of completed studies, school or vocational training
   - Enrolment confirmation from the university
   - Proof of funds: bank statements, **or** a guarantee from a solvent person living in Switzerland (who must also provide their latest tax bill and a debt-collection extract)
   - If self-financed: a Swiss bank or PostFinance confirmation of **at least CHF 2,000 per month**
   - Copy of a valid passport
   - All documents not in German must be translated
3. **Documents HSG provides for the visa** [S6]: a colour printout of the Zulassungsverfügung, the semester invoice plus payment confirmation, and an enrolment confirmation if required.
4. **Proof of funds for the visa** [S6]: bank statements showing at least **CHF 24,000**, in an account in your own name, at a Swiss bank or a bank with a Swiss branch, in German or English. You may also have to confirm that you will leave after your studies.
5. **Processing time:**
   - Official: "several weeks or even months" [S17]
   - City of St.Gallen: a visa takes "around three to five weeks" [S18]
   - Hong Kong consulate: at least 8–12 weeks [S19]
   - Swiss office in Taiwan: about 8–12 weeks [S30]

### First 14 days after arrival
6. **Register in person** at the Residents' Office, Rathaus, Poststrasse 28, within 14 days. Late registration is fined [S1, S6]. Bring a rental agreement or accommodation confirmation, your passport and travel documents, and your health-insurance card or policy [S1].
7. **Biometrics appointment** after the permit is approved [S20]:
   - Ausweisstelle, Oberer Graben 32, 9001 St.Gallen
   - Come only with the written invitation, and bring a valid passport
   - Photo, signature, and fingerprints if needed
   - Reschedule online with the 8-digit code from the invitation
   - Hours: Mon–Wed and Fri 08:00–11:30 / 13:00–17:00; Thu 10:00–11:30 / 13:30–19:00

### Within 3 months
8. **Health insurance** (see section 5).

### During studies
9. **Work** (see section 6).
10. **Extend the permit** at least 2 weeks before it expires. Your passport must be valid at least 3 months beyond the stay [S7]. The city says to apply about 2 months before expiry [S18].
11. **Lost permit card:** report it in person at a police station, then send the report to the Migrationsamt [S2].
12. **Driving licence:** exchange a foreign licence **within 12 months** at the Strassenverkehrsamt, or take a new Swiss test (info.stva@sg.ch, 058 229 22 22) [S21].

### After graduation
13. The permit ends **at the latest 6 months after graduation**. The total stay for study is max. 8 years [S9, S22].
14. Graduates may get a **6-month permit to look for a qualified job**, if they have enough money and accommodation. It cannot be extended [S9, S15].

## 3. Checklist: exchange students (1–2 semesters)

### Before arrival
1. Follow your HSG route from section 1 [S14].
2. Grant documents are emailed by **end of June** (autumn semester) or **mid-November** (spring semester) [S14].
3. Pay HSG back **CHF 96** for the grant, online, by credit card only, before arrival. It is owed even if you cancel [S14].

### First 14 days after arrival
4. Register at the Residents' Office within 14 days [S4].
   - Bring your passport, Form R, a copy of your rental agreement, and the grant (Zusicherung) if you are on the permit-grant route.
   - Pay **CHF 25** processing fee, plus the permit card fee:
     - **CHF 123** for Australia, Japan, Malaysia, New Zealand and Singapore
     - **CHF 122** for all other countries [S4]
5. Biometrics appointment at the Migrationsamt: pay **CHF 25** there. The card arrives by post in about 10 days [S4].
6. Upload Form R to Mobility-Online [S4].

### During and after
7. Permit type: **L** [S4].
8. Work: a visa is not a work permit. The employer must apply, and approval is "highly unlikely" [S4].
9. Health insurance: see section 5.
10. Before leaving: deregister at the Residents' Office (up to 1 month before departure) and upload the stamped Form D [S4, S5].

## 5. Health insurance [S25, S26, S27]
- Mandatory for everyone living in Switzerland for more than 3 months.
- Show proof of cover **within 3 months after arrival**, after registering at the Residents' Office.
- Non-EU students can be **exempted** in one of two ways:
  - **A Swiss student package (recommended):** Swisscare from CHF 38/month, Scorestudies from CHF 42/month. SWICA is CHF 155/month for ages 19–25.
  - **A home policy:** it must have no cap on medical costs and cover treatments, hospital and medication, and the insurer must sign and stamp page 2 of the form. Travel insurance is not accepted. HSG says this route is "rarely accepted".
- **Apply with:** the exemption form, the insurer's confirmation and your HSG enrolment confirmation. Submit in person at the Residents' Office or by email to **kvg@stadt.sg.ch**.
- The exemption lasts 3 years and can be extended by 3 more.

## 6. Working during studies (degree students) [S15, S9, S28]
- Max. **15 hours/week** during the semester, **at the earliest after 6 months** of study.
- **Exception:** a foreign Bachelor's holder doing a Master's in the same or a similar field may work at the university's own institute without the 6-month wait.
- **Apply to the Migrationsamt before starting work.** Changing jobs also needs approval.
- Full-time work in semester breaks is only allowed on application, with the university's written consent.
- More than 15 hours needs a work permit. It is checked for Swiss-worker priority, qualification, and pay and conditions.

---

## 7. Moving to St.Gallen from another Swiss canton
- If you hold a B or L permit from another canton, apply for a canton change **before moving**. L permit holders have no entitlement to a change [S8].
- Documents: Form A1, employment contract, a social-aid confirmation and a debt-collection extract from your last commune [S8].

---

## 8. Customs [S29]
Household goods are duty-free if you move your residence to Switzerland and have used them for at least 6 months. The source does not say whether a student stay counts as moving residence.
